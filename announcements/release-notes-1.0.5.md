## What's new

### Android app
- **The whole screen for your board**: open or closed, the edge tray floats over the board instead of keeping a strip clear down the right side. Scroll to the end and the last column comes out from under the open tray.
- **More room for the board's name**: the title at the top runs right up to the tray.
- **The sidebar slides on tablets**: folding to its rail and back, it eases instead of jumping, as on the website (new in 1.0.4 on computers).

### Windows, macOS and Linux apps
- **Narrow windows too**: below 720 pixels wide, the board takes the whole width under the tray in the same way.

### Website
- **On phones**, the board takes the whole width under the open tray in the same way.
