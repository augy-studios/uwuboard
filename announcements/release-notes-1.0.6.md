On a computer, everything you can press shows a pointing hand under the mouse, as it does on the website.

## Download

| Device | File |
|---|---|
| Android 7.0 or newer | `uwuboard-1.0.6.apk` |
| Windows 10 or 11 (x64 or Arm) | `uwuboard-1.0.6-setup.exe` (or the `.msi`, for installing on many computers at once) |
| Mac with Apple silicon (M1 and later) | `uwuboard-1.0.6-mac-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel processor | `uwuboard-1.0.6-mac-x64.dmg` |
| Debian, Ubuntu, Mint (x64 or arm64) | `uwuboard-1.0.6-amd64.deb` or `uwuboard-1.0.6-arm64.deb` |
| Fedora, openSUSE | `uwuboard-1.0.6-x86_64.rpm` or `uwuboard-1.0.6-aarch64.rpm` |
| Any other Linux | `uwuboard-1.0.6-x86_64.AppImage` or `uwuboard-1.0.6-aarch64.AppImage` |

Already have an earlier version? The apps update themselves: Android offers it on the Updates page, and the desktop apps offer **Restart to update**.

## What's new

### Windows, macOS and Linux apps
- **A pointing hand on what you can press**: buttons, menus, tabs, checkboxes, chips, colour swatches, your picture, the tray's handle and the window's own buttons all show the hand under the mouse instead of the arrow, on the welcome screen and in the widget window too.

### Android app
- **The same with a mouse**: on a tablet or Chromebook with a mouse, what you can press shows the pointing hand.

## What's fixed

### All apps
- **Report a bug, with a screen reader**: the boxes for what went wrong and how to reach you keep their names once there's text in them, instead of being read out as a nameless text box.
