The Windows, macOS and Linux apps can wear your system's own glass: Fluent 2 on Windows, macOS Tahoe on a Mac, and Deepin and COSMIC's frosted glass on Linux. This one is for computers only: Android stays on 1.0.6.

## What's new

### Windows, macOS and Linux apps
- **Your system's own glass**: in **Theme**, the second look beside UwU Apps is now the glass your computer draws, in its accent colour and font, as near to iOS's Liquid Glass as each system goes.
- **Fluent 2 on Windows**: Mica behind the window and Acrylic on what opens over it, in Segoe UI. On Windows 10, which has no Mica, uwuBoard frosts its own.
- **macOS Tahoe on a Mac**: your desktop through the window, rounded glass and capsule buttons. With **Reduce transparency** on, uwuBoard frosts its own.
- **Deepin (DDE) and COSMIC on Linux**: frosted glass, with the desktop blurred behind the window where the compositor can (DDE and KDE under X11).
- **Readable in any accent**: links, labels and buttons darken or lighten your accent colour until their words read clearly, yellow included.
- **Still UwU Apps by default**: the apps open in the UwU Apps look until you choose the glass. If you'd picked Material You before, you get the glass instead.
