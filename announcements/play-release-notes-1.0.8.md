# Google Play release notes, 1.0.8

Paste the block below into the release's **Release notes** box in Play Console (GOOGLE-PLAY.md,
section 10). Play allows 500 characters per language; this is about 300.

```text
<en-GB>
What's new in 1.0.8:
• Card descriptions formatted as on Discord: bold, italic, spoilers, lists, links and more
• A card moved into Done is marked completed that day
• On a Timeline, work that started late shows as it really went
• On Android 15 and later, a sheet's buttons no longer hide behind the navigation bar
</en-GB>
```

The Google Play build has no Updates page of its own (Play updates it), so its notes leave that out.
