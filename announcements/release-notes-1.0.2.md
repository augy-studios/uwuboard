## What's new

### Everywhere
- **Report a bug**: in the edge tray. Say what went wrong, and leave a way to reach you if you'd like an answer.
- **Screenshot a page**: go to the page with the bug and take it whole, every column top to bottom. Or add up to three pictures from your device.
- **A report ID to quote**: once it's sent, you get an ID like 20261114-7K3Q, so the report can be talked about by it.
- **Only what helps**: the app's version, your device and the kind of page you were on, all shown before it's sent. Never a board's or a card's name.
