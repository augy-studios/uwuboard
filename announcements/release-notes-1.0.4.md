The sidebar in the Windows, macOS and Linux apps folds and unfolds smoothly, as it does on the website. This one is for computers only: Android stays on 1.0.3.

## Download

| Device | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11 (x64 or Arm) | `uwuboard-1.0.4-setup.exe` (or the `.msi`, for installing on many computers at once) |
| Mac with Apple silicon (M1 and later) | `uwuboard-1.0.4-mac-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel processor | `uwuboard-1.0.4-mac-x64.dmg` |
| Debian, Ubuntu, Mint (x64 or arm64) | `uwuboard-1.0.4-amd64.deb` or `uwuboard-1.0.4-arm64.deb` |
| Fedora, openSUSE | `uwuboard-1.0.4-x86_64.rpm` or `uwuboard-1.0.4-aarch64.rpm` |

Already have 1.0.3 on a computer? It updates itself: look for **Restart to update**.

## What's new

### Windows, macOS and Linux apps
- **The sidebar slides**: folding to its rail of initials and back, it eases over a quarter of a second instead of jumping, as on the website. With animations turned off in your system's settings, it still snaps.
