✨NEW✨ uwuBoard 1.0.0: now an app
board.uwuapps.org

Android app:
- Every screen built for Android: quick to open, smooth to drag cards
- Material You by default: your phone's font and wallpaper colours
- The UwU Apps look is a switch away in Theme
- An Updates page with what's new in every version
- A notification the moment a new version is out, if you'd like one

Windows, macOS and Linux apps:
- A command bar (Ctrl+K) to search every board and card
- Lives in the tray and tells you when a card is due
- Desktop widgets, a tray countdown, and your boards in the Jump List and Dock
- Updates itself

Your boards come with you: sign in, or Sync them across as a guest.

Download the apps and view patch notes: github.com/augy-studios/uwuboard/releases/latest
