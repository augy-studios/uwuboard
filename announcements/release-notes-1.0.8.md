## What's new

### All apps
- **Descriptions formatted as on Discord**: bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, code, spoilers, headings, small text, quotes, lists and links, written the way Discord has them. A card shows the first two lines of its description, formatted, under its title, with spoilers kept hidden.
- **A toolbar to format with**: press a description to edit it, and use the buttons above it, or Ctrl+B, I and U (⌘ on a Mac). Leave the box and it's shown formatted again.
- **Completed dates**: a card moved into the board's last column (Done) is marked completed that day, and the mark goes if it's moved back out. On a Timeline you can change the date yourself.
- **Late work, as it went**: on a Timeline a task can start after its due date. Its bar then shows the work itself as a solid stripe from its start to the day it was completed (or to today while it's still going), with a red line at the due date it ran past. Such a bar moves whole, every date with it.

## What's fixed

### Android app
- **Sheets on Android 15 and later**: the buttons at the bottom of a sheet, such as **Save** on a card, are no longer hidden behind the navigation bar.

### All apps
- **Late, but done**: a task that's completed is no longer marked late on the Timeline.
