A new look for the Updates page, and fixes for the tours, the tray and the desktop apps' windows.

## Download

| Device | File |
|---|---|
| Android 7.0 or newer | `uwuboard-1.0.1.apk` |
| Windows 10 or 11 (x64 or Arm) | `uwuboard-1.0.1-setup.exe` (or the `.msi`, for installing on many computers at once) |
| Mac with Apple silicon (M1 and later) | `uwuboard-1.0.1-mac-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel processor | `uwuboard-1.0.1-mac-x64.dmg` |
| Debian, Ubuntu, Mint (x64 or arm64) | `uwuboard-1.0.1-amd64.deb` or `uwuboard-1.0.1-arm64.deb` |
| Fedora, openSUSE | `uwuboard-1.0.1-x86_64.rpm` or `uwuboard-1.0.1-aarch64.rpm` |
| Any other Linux | `uwuboard-1.0.1-x86_64.AppImage` or `uwuboard-1.0.1-aarch64.AppImage` |

Already have 1.0.0? The apps update themselves: Android offers it on the Updates page, and the desktop apps offer **Restart to update**.

## What's new

### Android app
- **Updates page, redrawn**: every version on a timeline, its changes in cards by kind, the newest open by itself, as Android's own updater shows them.
- **Wavy progress**: checking for and downloading an update show Material You's wavy progress bar.

### Windows, macOS and Linux apps
- **Tours light up the right thing**: the spotlight no longer sits below its target by the height of the title bar.
- **No more grey bar**: hovering over the title bar's buttons shows their names again instead of a grey bar across the top.
- **Right-click menus open at the pointer**, not below it.
- **Make it yours from the tray** opens at its proper size, and the boards' window comes back as it was, even when it was maximised.
- **The tray icon's menu** looks like the rest of Windows 11, dark in dark mode, and closes when you click somewhere else.

### Everywhere
- **Tours stay on screen**: the card moves above, below or beside what it's about, wherever there's room, and the thing being shown scrolls into view first.
