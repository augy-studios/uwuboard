# Google Play release notes, 1.0.5

Paste the block below into the release's **Release notes** box in Play Console (GOOGLE-PLAY.md,
section 10). Play allows 500 characters per language; this is about 190.

```text
<en-GB>
What's new in 1.0.5:
• Your board gets the whole screen: the edge tray floats over it, open or closed
• The sidebar slides open and closed on tablets
• Smaller fixes and improvements
</en-GB>
```

The Google Play build has no Updates page of its own (Play updates it), so its notes leave that out.
