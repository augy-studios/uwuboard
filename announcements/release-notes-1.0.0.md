uwuBoard is now an app: for Android, in Material You by default, and for Windows, macOS and Linux, with everything the website does and more.

## Download

| Device | File |
|---|---|
| Android 7.0 or newer | `uwuboard-1.0.0.apk` |
| Windows 10 or 11 (x64 or Arm) | `uwuboard-1.0.0-setup.exe` (or the `.msi`, for installing on many computers at once) |
| Mac with Apple silicon (M1 and later) | `uwuboard-1.0.0-mac-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel processor | `uwuboard-1.0.0-mac-x64.dmg` |
| Debian, Ubuntu, Mint (x64 or arm64) | `uwuboard-1.0.0-amd64.deb` or `uwuboard-1.0.0-arm64.deb` |
| Fedora, openSUSE | `uwuboard-1.0.0-x86_64.rpm` or `uwuboard-1.0.0-aarch64.rpm` |
| Any other Linux | `uwuboard-1.0.0-x86_64.AppImage` or `uwuboard-1.0.0-aarch64.AppImage` |

## Installing

**Android**: download the `.apk` on your phone and open it. If Android asks, allow installing apps from your browser or Files. If Play Protect asks about the app, tap **More details** > **Install anyway**.

**Windows**: run `uwuboard-1.0.0-setup.exe`. It's not signed with a paid certificate, so SmartScreen may warn the first time: **More info** > **Run anyway**. It installs for you alone, no administrator needed.

**Mac**: open the `.dmg` and drag uwuBoard to Applications. The first time, right-click the app > **Open**. If macOS says it's damaged, run `xattr -cr /Applications/uwuBoard.app` in Terminal.

**Linux**: `sudo apt install ./uwuboard-1.0.0-amd64.deb`, or `sudo dnf install ./uwuboard-1.0.0-x86_64.rpm`. For the AppImage, make it executable and run it.

Your boards come with you: sign in with your uwuBoard account, or as a guest bring them across with **Sync** or a board file.

## What's new

### Android app
- **A real app**: every screen built for Android, quick to open and smooth to drag cards around.
- **Material You by default**: your phone's font and, on Android 12 and later, your wallpaper's colours. The UwU Apps look you know from the website, with its brand colours and the Jua font, is one switch away in **Theme**.
- **Updates page**: what's new in this version and every one before it, and **Update** downloads the next version, checks it and installs it.
- **New versions of the app**: turn it on in the Updates page for a notification the moment a new version is out, even with the app closed.
- **Board links open in the app**: a sync link or QR code from another device opens straight in uwuBoard.
- **Export to Downloads**: a board file saves straight into your phone's Downloads.

### Windows, macOS and Linux apps
- **Make it yours**: the first window picks the theme, the brand colour (or your computer's own accent colour) and whether uwuBoard starts when you sign in.
- **Its own title bar**, with back and forward through the boards you've opened, and a **command bar** (`Ctrl+K`): search every board and card, run any command, or paste a link.
- **Boards beside each other**: on a wide window the boards stay in a list on the left while the open one fills the rest.
- **Lives in the tray**: closing the window keeps uwuBoard running, so it can tell you when a card falls due today or goes overdue.
- **Tray countdown**: right-click a board and pick **Set as Tray Countdown** to see its next due cards in the tray's tooltip (and beside the menu bar icon on a Mac).
- **Desktop widgets**: pin a board's due cards to your desktop, in a small window you can keep on top.
- **Your boards in the Jump List and the Dock**, and the taskbar's thumbnail buttons step through them.
- **Screenshots**, including a long one of the whole board, copied and saved to Downloads.
- **Right-click menus**, keyboard shortcuts and, on a Mac, the menu bar.
- **Updates itself**: it checks as it opens and every hour, downloads the update and offers **Restart to update**.

### Everywhere
- **Everything the website does**: Kanban boards and Timelines, cards with due dates, priorities, labels, checklists and images, guest mode or an account, Sync between devices, board files, tours and the edge tray, under the same keys, so nothing needs converting.
