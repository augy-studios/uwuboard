# Google Play release notes, 1.0.0

Paste the block below into the release's **Release notes** box in Play Console (GOOGLE-PLAY.md,
section 10). Play allows 500 characters per language; this is about 390. The tag must match a
language the store listing has: the app is in English only, so the listing's default language is
English (United Kingdom), `en-GB`. If Play shows the tag as one it doesn't know, change it to the
one Play lists.

```text
<en-GB>
uwuBoard, the Kanban way, now as an app.
• Kanban boards and Timelines: drag cards from To Do to Done, or lay tasks out across dates
• Cards with due dates, priorities, labels, checklists and images
• Material You by default, with the UwU Apps look a switch away in Theme
• Use it as a guest, or sign in and your boards follow you
• Sync boards straight to another device with a code
</en-GB>
```

Play shows its own update notices, so the Google Play build has no Updates page bar of its own
and doesn't list the GitHub releases' notes.
