The Windows, macOS and Linux apps show your boards again, and Long screenshot takes the whole board again. Nothing changes on Android.

## Download

| Device | File |
|---|---|
| Android 7.0 or newer | `uwuboard-1.0.3.apk` |
| Windows 10 or 11 (x64 or Arm) | `uwuboard-1.0.3-setup.exe` (or the `.msi`, for installing on many computers at once) |
| Mac with Apple silicon (M1 and later) | `uwuboard-1.0.3-mac-arm64.dmg` |
| Mac with an Intel processor | `uwuboard-1.0.3-mac-x64.dmg` |
| Debian, Ubuntu, Mint (x64 or arm64) | `uwuboard-1.0.3-amd64.deb` or `uwuboard-1.0.3-arm64.deb` |
| Fedora, openSUSE | `uwuboard-1.0.3-x86_64.rpm` or `uwuboard-1.0.3-aarch64.rpm` |
| Any other Linux | `uwuboard-1.0.3-x86_64.AppImage` or `uwuboard-1.0.3-aarch64.AppImage` |

On 1.0.2 on a computer? The window stays black until you update, but the update still comes: a notification and the tray icon offer **Restart to update**, and it's installed when you quit anyway. Installed from the `.msi`, `.deb` or `.rpm`? Download the new one from the table above.

## What's fixed

### Windows, macOS and Linux apps
- **No more black window**: 1.0.2 drew nothing under the title bar. Your boards are back.
- **Long screenshot** takes the whole board again, instead of a corner of it on black.
