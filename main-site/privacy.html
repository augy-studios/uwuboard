Privacy

Last updated 15 November 2026

uwuBoard is made by Augy Studios. This page says what uwuBoard keeps about you, where it goes, and how to delete it. It covers the website at board.uwuapps.org, the Android app (from Google Play or GitHub), and the Windows, macOS and Linux apps.

The short version

  • No ads, and nothing about you is sold. The apps don't use your device's advertising ID and have no analytics or tracking.
  • As a guest, your boards stay on your device. Nothing is sent to us.
  • With an account, we keep your username, email address, a scrambled form of your password and your boards, so you can open them anywhere.
  • Delete Account removes all of it, straight away.
  • The website (not the apps) uses Google Analytics to count visits.

1. Using uwuBoard as a guest

As a guest, you don't need an account. Your boards, columns, cards, pictures, theme and settings are kept on your device only: in your browser's storage on the website, and in the app's own storage in the apps. They aren't sent to us. To move them, you can export a board to a file yourself, or use Sync (section 3).

2. With an account

An account keeps your boards on uwuBoard's server, so you can open them on any device. We keep:

  • Your username and email address, given when you register. The email address is only used to keep accounts apart: we don't send you email.
  • Your password, only in a scrambled form (a bcrypt hash), which can't be turned back into the password.
  • A display name and a profile picture, if you add them.
  • Your boards: their columns, cards, dates, labels, checklists and the pictures you add to cards.
  • A sign-in token for each device you sign in on, so you stay signed in. It lasts 30 days, and ends when you sign out.

We use these only to run your account and show you your boards. Your card pictures and profile picture are private: they're only shown to you, through uwuBoard's server, once you're signed in.

3. Sync with another device

Sync copies boards straight from one of your devices to another, with a code or QR code, account or not. To connect the two devices:

  • A free public service, PeerJS (0.peerjs.com), introduces them. It sees both devices' IP addresses and a random code, but not your boards.
  • Google's and Cloudflare's STUN servers tell each device its own public address. They see your IP address.

Your boards then go straight from one device to the other, encrypted on the way (WebRTC with DTLS). They don't pass through uwuBoard's server.

4. Report a bug

If you use Report a bug (in the edge tray), what you send goes to uwuBoard's server and on to Augy Studios as a message from a Telegram bot. It contains:

  • what you typed, and a way to reach you, if you give one;
  • up to three pictures, if you add them: from your device, or a screenshot of a page of the app. A screenshot shows whatever is on that page, which can include your boards;
  • what helps find the bug, all shown to you before it's sent: the app's version, which app (website, Android, desktop), your device and system as your browser or app describes them, and the kind of page you were on. Never a board's or a card's name, unless a picture you add shows it.

uwuBoard's server doesn't store reports. They're kept in Augy Studios' Telegram chats, which Telegram stores under its own privacy policy. To limit misuse, the server counts reports from each IP address, for a minute and, where set up, for an hour; then the count is gone. Each report gets an ID (like 20261114-7K3Q): quote it to have that report deleted.

5. Updates and notifications

  • The Android app from GitHub asks uwuBoard's server whether there's a new version, and downloads it from GitHub, which sees your IP address.
  • "New versions of the app" (Android, off unless you turn it on) uses Firebase Cloud Messaging, Google's push service. Your phone joins a topic for uwuBoard's new versions, and Google handles the address it sends notifications to. uwuBoard's server keeps no list of phones.
  • The Android app from Google Play is updated by Google Play, and has no updater of its own.
  • The Windows, macOS and Linux apps check GitHub for new versions themselves, about once an hour.

6. The website

  • Google Analytics counts visits to the website's pages: which pages are opened, roughly where from, and on what kind of device and browser. It uses cookies to tell visits apart. It's on the website only, not in the apps. Your browser's settings or an ad blocker can stop it, and uwuBoard works the same without it.
  • Google Fonts serves the website's font, so Google sees your IP address when it loads. The apps carry the font themselves.
  • For Sync, the website loads the PeerJS library from cdnjs (Cloudflare).
  • Buy Augy a Coffee opens Stripe's page, only if you click it. What you give there is handled by Stripe.

7. Android permissions

  • Internet: for your account, Sync and Report a bug.
  • Notifications: only asked for when you turn on "New versions of the app".
  • Install apps: the GitHub version only, to install its own updates when you choose Update. The Google Play version doesn't have it.
  • Storage: on Android 9 and older only, to save a board you export to Downloads.

uwuBoard doesn't use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or advertising ID.

9. Keeping and deleting your data

Your account and boards are kept until you delete them. To delete your account and everything in it:

  • In the apps: open the sidebar, your picture, Profile, then Delete Account.
  • On the website: sign in at board.uwuapps.org, click your picture, then Delete Account.

You'll be asked for your password. Then your card pictures, your profile picture, your sign-in tokens, your boards and your account are all deleted from uwuBoard's server straight away. This can't be undone, so export any board you want to keep first.

Can't sign in? Email augy@augystudios.com from the address on your account, and we'll delete it for you.

Guest boards are deleted by clearing the website's data in your browser, or by uninstalling the app. Bug reports can be deleted on request: quote the report's ID.

10. Your choices and rights

You can use uwuBoard without an account, and leave out the display name, profile picture and contact details in bug reports. You can change your display name, password and picture in Profile, and export any board to a file.

Depending on where you live (for example under the UK or EU GDPR, or Singapore's PDPA), you may have the right to see the data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, take a copy, or object to how it's used. Email us to ask, and we'll answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

11. Keeping it safe

Everything between the apps and uwuBoard's server is sent over HTTPS, and Sync is encrypted from device to device. Passwords are stored only as hashes. Pictures are kept private, and the keys to the database stay on the server. No service is perfectly secure, but if something goes wrong that affects your data, we'll tell you.

12. Children

uwuBoard isn't made for children under 13, and we don't knowingly keep their data. If you think a child under 13 has made an account, email us and we'll delete it.

13. Changes to this page

If what uwuBoard does with your data changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top says when. Bigger changes are also said in the apps' release notes.
